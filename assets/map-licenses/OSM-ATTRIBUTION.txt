This offline map is generated from an OpenStreetMap regional extract using
tilemaker. The accompanying palestine.manifest.json records the exact source,
source data timestamp, checksums, generator, and generation configuration.
Source data is available through the sourceUrl recorded in that manifest.
The generation scripts are in the PalMesh source repository, scripts/maps/.

Map features describe the source database at its recorded data timestamp.
They do not verify current road access, facility operation, damage or safety.
